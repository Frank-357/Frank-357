# Prompts for AI-generated art (optional)

The banner and diagrams in this folder are hand-built SVGs (`scripts/build_assets.py`). No image
model was used. If you'd like an AI-painted background later, paste one of these into Gemini
(or any image model), then save the result as `assets/banner-bg.png` and swap it into the README.

Tips that apply to every prompt:
- Ask for **no text in the image**. Image models misspell names. Keep the name and titles as
  SVG text on top, or add them in Figma or Keynote.
- Ask for a wide image (about 3.2 : 1, e.g. 1856 × 576, the same size as Prof. Barry's banner).
- Leave the left 55% dark and empty so the text stays readable.
- Don't use the University of Chicago crest or any firm logos in generated art. Those are
  trademarks; the text wordmark in the SVG is enough.

## 1. Banner background (closest to Prof. Barry's style)

> Ultra-wide 3.2:1 abstract banner background, no text, no logos. Deep midnight navy on the left
> fading to dark crimson / maroon (#800000) on the right. From a single bright point at the right
> edge, hundreds of fine glowing fiber-optic light threads fan out to the left, thin and soft pink-white,
> with a few tiny glowing particles along them. Very faint, barely visible candlestick chart
> silhouettes along the bottom edge. The left 55% is mostly dark negative space for text. Elegant,
> minimal, high-end financial-technology aesthetic, cinematic lighting, 8k, sharp.

## 2. Alternative: factor-research lattice

> Ultra-wide 3.2:1 abstract background, no text. A dark navy field with a subtle 3D lattice of tiny
> glowing nodes, like a correlation matrix seen in perspective, fading into depth on the right side.
> A few nodes glow brighter in crimson; thin lines connect some of them. Quiet, precise, academic,
> quantitative-research mood. Left half mostly empty and dark.

## 3. Alternative: time-series ribbons

> Ultra-wide 3.2:1 abstract background, no text. Several translucent flowing ribbons that look like
> smoothed financial time series, layered in navy, steel blue and maroon, moving left to right across
> a near-black background, with soft depth of field. Minimal, modern, premium. Left half darker and
> calmer for overlaid text.

## 4. Square avatar background (optional)

> Square 1:1 abstract avatar background, no text, no face. Dark navy to maroon radial gradient with a
> few fine glowing light threads converging to the center. Minimal and clean, so it stays legible as a
> small circle.
